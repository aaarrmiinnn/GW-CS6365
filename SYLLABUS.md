## Course Outline

### Part 1: Machine Learning with Graphs (Weeks 1–5)
- Graph Theory & Machine Learning: Introduction to graph theory fundamentals and their applications in machine learning, including graph neural networks.  
- Graph-Based Information Retrieval: Advanced techniques in using graph structures for information retrieval and their practical implications.  
- Applications: Exploration of graph theory in web information retrieval, recommendation systems, and other domains with real-world case studies.  

### Part 2: Recommendation Systems (Weeks 6–10)
- Overview & Types: Introduction to various recommendation system architectures including content-based, collaborative, and hybrid models.  
- Techniques & Evaluation: Exploration of matrix factorization, deep learning approaches, and evaluation metrics.  
- Applications: Use cases in industry (e.g., Comcast, Netflix, Amazon), with focus on scalability and ethical considerations.  

### Part 3: Search and Information Retrieval (Weeks 11–14)
- Foundations: Core principles of search engines and information retrieval (e.g., PageRank, BM25).  
- Advanced Techniques: Semantic search, personalization, advanced indexing, and query processing.  
- Applications: Real-world applications including retrieval-augmented generation (RAGs).
